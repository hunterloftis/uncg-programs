Notes on the data and boxes
  • Left and right edges show the 25th and 75th earnings percentiles. The vertical mark shows the median. The lowest and highest 25% of earners are not drawn.
  • Colored marks identify four extreme box edges: red for the lowest floor and ceiling, green for the highest. Floor means the 25th percentile (solid line); ceiling means the 75th percentile (dashed line). These are quartile edges, not minimum or maximum individual earnings. Extremes use the entries drawn on this chart.
  • Box height is proportional to the full count of graduates with NC wages. It is not the count within the middle 50%. Box area does not represent a count, and the fill does not imply an even income distribution.
  • Each entry occupies one row, sorted by median earnings from lowest to highest. All UNCG boxes use the same count-to-height scale. High school reference boxes have fixed height; their survey samples cannot be compared as graduation-cohort counts. Survey sample counts appear in the tooltip.
  • NC TOWER reports nominal annual wages from NC unemployment-insurance records. Work outside NC, federal jobs, and self-employment are excluded. Graduates may still be enrolled in further education.
  • The UNCG cohort is labeled 2019 in NC TOWER; earnings cover the fourth year after graduation, in nominal dollars. It differs from the federal earnings sample used in the earlier chart.
  • High school quartiles and medians are calculated together from 2019–2023 ACS public microdata: ages 25–34, regular high school diploma or GED only, civilian employed, no school attendance in the prior three months, and positive annual earnings. Both NC and national estimates use the same filters.
  • Earnings include wages and net self-employment earnings (PERNP), adjusted to 2023 dollars with ADJINC. Percentiles use Census person weights (PWGTP): each is the first earnings value at which cumulative weight reaches 25%, 50%, or 75%. Values are rounded to the dollar. They are survey estimates; confidence intervals are not shown.
  • These Census estimates replace the earlier federal high school median references. No claim is made that they reproduce the federal benchmark calculation. Populations and measurement periods differ from the UNCG cohort. NC TOWER nominal wages and Census 2023 dollars share the dollar axis without a further inflation conversion.
  • Missing values are privacy-suppressed, not zero. The table retains all 63 six-digit program records. Some codes still combine degrees; a student with two majors may appear in both programs.
  • Names use UNCG degree inventories, with historical names and combined degrees noted. These historical programs are not a list of all degrees offered today.
