Notes on the data and boxes
    • Left and right edges show the 25th and 75th earnings percentiles. The vertical mark shows the median. The lowest and highest 25% of earners are not drawn.
    • Colored marks identify four extreme box edges among UNCG degree programs: red for the lowest floor and ceiling, green for the highest. Floor means the 25th percentile (solid line); ceiling means the 75th percentile (dashed line). These are quartile edges, not minimum or maximum individual earnings. Extremes compare UNCG program boxes only; high school references are excluded.
    • Box height is proportional to the full earnings sample count. It is not the count within the middle 50%. Box area does not represent a count, and the fill does not imply an even income distribution.
    • Each entry occupies one row, sorted by median earnings from lowest to highest. All UNCG boxes use the same count-to-height scale. High school reference boxes have fixed height; their survey samples cannot be compared as graduation-cohort counts. Survey sample counts appear in the tooltip.
    • PSEO covers UNCG graduates, not just federal-aid recipients. Earnings come from covered jobs nationwide, including most state and local government jobs and some federal jobs. Coverage excludes unincorporated self-employment, military service, and some other work. Graduates may still be enrolled in school.
    • The box cohort graduated in 2016–2018. Year five is the fifth calendar year following each graduation year, so box earnings are measured in 2021–2023 and adjusted to 2023 dollars. This is the latest UNCG cohort with published five-year outcomes in release R2026Q2.
    • The earnings sample requires positive covered earnings in at least three quarters and annual earnings at least equivalent to full-time work at the federal minimum wage. It does not establish full-time employment.
    • The UNCG residual category includes no observed employment and marginal employment: graduates who do not meet those earnings-sample requirements. It can include people in school, working in excluded jobs, or earning below the threshold. It is not an unemployment rate, and placement at $0 does not mean all had zero earnings.
    • Residual counts have lower fidelity than the earnings boxes: PSEO publishes them only by two-digit broad field, while earnings boxes use four-digit program groups. Each purple box in a field uses the same residual-to-employed ratio. This is a shared field ratio, not a measured employment ratio for the individual major. Tooltips show the field totals; do not add totals repeated on several rows.
    • For UNCG, purple height equals the blue box height multiplied by the field residual count divided by the field employed count. A 50% field residual share means equal residual and employed counts, so the boxes have equal heights. Purple height is not an observed program-level count. Both ratio counts come from the 2016–2018 employment file.
    • Purple boxes extend from $0 to the earnings box's 25th-percentile edge only to join the marks. Their width and area do not represent an income distribution or a count. Some people represented by purple earned money. A UNCG purple box is omitted when field counts are suppressed or when the row has only a pooled median circle.
    • PSEO uses privacy protection that adds noise to counts and earnings. Earnings-file and employment-file counts are protected separately and may differ. Residual shares use both counts from the same employment-file row; we do not subtract an earnings count from IPEDS graduations to estimate residuals. Suppressed values remain missing.
    • High school estimates use 2019–2023 ACS public microdata: civilians aged 25–34, regular high school diploma or GED only, and no school attendance in the prior three months. The base includes employed people, unemployed people, and people outside the labor force. Both NC and national estimates use the same filters.
    • High school earnings boxes cover annual earnings at or above $15,080 in 2023 dollars. The fixed cutoff is 40 hours × 52 weeks × $7.25, used here as an earnings benchmark, not proof of full-time work. Purple covers annual earnings below that cutoff, including zero and negative net earnings. Each person belongs to exactly one group; current employment status does not determine the group.
    • High school purple height equals the fixed earnings box height multiplied by the weighted population below the cutoff divided by the weighted qualifying population. Tooltips show population estimates, their share of the full base, and survey record counts. These counts are estimates of people, not graduation cohorts.
    • Earnings include wages and net self-employment earnings (PERNP), adjusted to 2023 dollars with ADJINC. Percentiles use Census person weights (PWGTP): each is the first earnings value at which cumulative weight reaches 25%, 50%, or 75%. Values are rounded to the dollar. They are survey estimates; confidence intervals are not shown.
    • ACS no/low earnings is an approximation, not PSEO's residual measure. ACS includes self-employment earnings and does not identify covered earnings by quarter. The fixed cutoff applies after adjustment to 2023 dollars; it does not reproduce PSEO's year-specific threshold. These sources describe different populations and periods and do not measure the causal effect of earning a UNCG degree.
    • PSEO earnings use four-digit program groups, which may combine several degrees. Names list degree matches from the UNCG inventory; quartiles apply to the whole reporting group, not separately to each listed degree. Graduates with multiple degrees can appear more than once. Do not sum rows to obtain a campus total.
    • These are historical program outcomes, not a list of every degree offered today or a prediction of an individual student's earnings. The table keeps all source program groups, including suppressed groups.
    • Rows are sorted by the displayed median earnings from lowest to highest, including pooled-cohort circles. Programs without either median are omitted from the chart. Missing counts are never drawn as zero.
