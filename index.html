Notes on the data and boxes
    • Box edges show the 25th and 75th earnings percentiles; the line shows the median among qualifying earners.
    • Red marks show the lowest UNCG quartiles; green marks show the highest. Floor means the 25th percentile (solid line); ceiling means the 75th percentile (dashed line). These are not minimum or maximum individual earnings.
    • Box height is proportional to the full earnings sample count. It is not the count within the middle 50%. Box area does not represent a count, and the fill does not imply an even income distribution.
    • All UNCG boxes use the same count-to-height scale. High school survey samples cannot be compared as graduation-cohort counts.
    • PSEO covers UNCG graduates, not just federal-aid recipients. Earnings come from covered jobs nationwide, including most state and local government jobs and some federal jobs. Coverage excludes unincorporated self-employment, military service, and some other work. Graduates may still be enrolled in school.
    • The box cohort graduated in 2016–2018. Year five is the fifth calendar year following each graduation year, so box earnings are measured in 2021–2023 and adjusted to 2023 dollars. This is the latest UNCG cohort with published five-year outcomes in release R2026Q2.
    • The earnings sample requires positive covered earnings in at least three quarters and annual earnings at least equivalent to full-time work at the federal minimum wage. It does not establish full-time employment.
    • The UNCG residual category includes no observed employment and marginal employment: graduates who do not meet those earnings-sample requirements. It can include people in school, working in excluded jobs, or earning below the threshold. It is not an unemployment rate and does not mean all had zero earnings.
    • PSEO publishes employment counts by two-digit broad field and earnings by four-digit program group. The employment share applies to the field, not each major.
    • The UNCG share employed above threshold equals the field employed count divided by the sum of its residual and employed counts. Both counts come from the 2016–2018 employment file, including beside pooled median circles. A dash means the field counts are suppressed.
    • PSEO adds noise to protect privacy. Earnings-file and employment-file counts are protected separately and may differ. Employment shares use counts from the same employment-file row.
    • High school estimates use 2019–2023 ACS public microdata: civilians aged 25–34, regular high school diploma or GED only, and no school attendance in the prior three months. The base includes employed people, unemployed people, and people outside the labor force. Both NC and national estimates use the same filters.
    • High school earnings boxes cover wage-and-salary income of at least $12,687.50 before inflation adjustment. The cutoff is 35 hours × 50 weeks × $7.25, following the PSEO technical guide (June 2023). The federal minimum wage was $7.25 throughout 2019–2023. The below-cutoff group covers wages below the cutoff, including zero. “Above earnings cutoff” includes earnings exactly at the cutoff. Each person belongs to exactly one group; current employment status does not determine the group. The threshold does not establish full-time employment.
    • The high school share equals the weighted population at or above the cutoff divided by the full weighted reference population. These counts estimate people, not graduation cohorts.
    • High school earnings use wage-and-salary income (WAGP), excluding self-employment income. People with only self-employment income have zero wages and belong to the no/low wage earnings group. Eligibility uses WAGP before inflation adjustment; qualifying wages are then adjusted to 2023 dollars with ADJINC for the earnings quartiles. Percentiles use Census person weights (PWGTP): each is the first earnings value at which cumulative weight reaches 25%, 50%, or 75%. Values are rounded to the dollar. They are survey estimates; confidence intervals are not shown.
    • The ACS share above the wage cutoff approximates PSEO’s earnings eligibility measure. ACS cannot identify earnings in each calendar quarter or reproduce PSEO's job coverage. It measures income over the prior 12 months rather than a calendar year. No quarterly or weeks-worked test is imposed. These sources describe different populations and periods and do not measure the causal effect of earning a UNCG degree.
    • PSEO earnings use four-digit program groups, which may combine several degrees. Names list degree matches from the UNCG inventory; quartiles apply to the whole reporting group, not separately to each listed degree. Graduates with multiple degrees can appear more than once. Do not sum rows to obtain a campus total.
    • These historical outcomes do not predict an individual student's earnings or list every degree offered today. Tables include suppressed program groups; the chart omits groups without a published median.
